Setup Guide

Step 1: Create Google Cloud Project

  1. Go to Google Cloud Console
  2. Create a new project named "Auto Gmail Replier"
  3. Enable the Gmail API
  4. Go to "Credentials" and create an "OAuth 2.0 Client ID" (Web application)
  5. Add redirect URI: https://yourwebsite.com/callback

Step 2: Configure Credentials

Your credentials are stored locally in your browser and never sent to external servers.

Step 3: Authenticate Gmail

Once you save your credentials, click "Login with Gmail" to authorize the app to access your emails.
